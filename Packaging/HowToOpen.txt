━━ English ━━
1. Drag Multi-Finder into the Applications folder.
2. Open it. Because this is a free indie app that isn't notarized by Apple, macOS says it
   "could not verify" the app. Click Done.
3. Open System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click "Open Anyway" next to
   "Multi-Finder was blocked". Confirm with your password. You only do this once.
Optional: to remove leftovers inside other apps' data folders, or to undo a Move to Trash
with ⌘Z, turn on Multi-Finder in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Full Disk Access.
Requires macOS 14 or later. Categories (on-device AI) needs macOS 26 with Apple Intelligence.
Nothing is ever deleted outright: files only go to the Trash, after you confirm.

macOS 14 以降。自動分類（オンデバイス AI）には macOS 26 と Apple Intelligence が必要です。

━━ 简体中文 ━━
1. 将 Multi-Finder 拖到“应用程序”文件夹。
2. 打开时，由于这是未经 Apple 公证的免费个人开发应用，macOS 会提示“无法验证”。点按“完成”。
3. 打开 系统设置 › 隐私与安全性，向下滚动，在有关 Multi-Finder 已被阻止的提示旁点按“仍要打开”，
   并输入密码确认。只需操作一次。
可选：若要删除其他应用数据文件夹中的残留，或用 ⌘Z 撤销移到废纸篓，请在
系统设置 › 隐私与安全性 › 完全磁盘访问权限 中打开 Multi-Finder。
需要 macOS 14 或更高版本。自动分类（设备端 AI）需要 macOS 26 和 Apple 智能。
不会直接删除任何文件：确认后只会移到废纸篓。
